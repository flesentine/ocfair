🥙 Easy ordering rule

Start with vegetarian, chicken, corn, fries, fruit and dessert options. Ask about pork/bacon and shared preparation when it matters. Don’t turn dinner into a research project.

✓ Good targets: street corn · agua fresca · chocolate strawberries · plant-based/vegetarian choices

⚠ Ask when needed: shared grills/fryers or stands serving bacon/sausage alongside your order

🌙 Exit rule

You do not need to stay until midnight. If the last hour feels like work, take the photo, let Shylene cast the final vote, and leave on a high note.
